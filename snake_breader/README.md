# Problem
You are in charge of keeping a snake breader system. The breader accepts an unlimited amount of snakes.
Two snakes can reproduce if their sex is oposite and if they are not family (i.e. Father-Daugther, Mohter-Son, GrandMother-Son. etc).

Snakes can be obtained in 2 ways
- By reproducing 2 snakes that are already part of the breader
- By buying them (aka. a new snake). This bougth snakes are garateed to have no relationship with the snakes in the breeder. 

Your task is to implement a breeder class. Include 2 methods: add_snake() and breed()
